# Walk-in checkout and purchase entry

- POS `/pos/sale` accepts an omitted or `null` `customerId` as **Walk-in**, persisted as `null` without creating or selecting a customer record. Explicit customer IDs must resolve to an available customer. A `400 customer_unavailable` tells the cashier to choose another customer or explicitly switch to Walk-in; cart and tender remain intact. Existing uncertain checkout requests retain their original retry ID. Non-POS sales still require a customer.
- **+ New Supplier** uses the existing supplier form, authenticated endpoint, duplicate-name check and validators. It keeps New Purchase open and selects the returned supplier. **+ New Item** uses the existing permission-protected quick item endpoint, selects the returned ID directly and focuses Purchase Quantity. Inventory-only items hide sale price and initialize it to zero; receiving never makes them sellable.
- **Save Order** saves the selected Ordered/Pending state without adding stock. **Save & Receive** saves that order first, then calls its existing transactional receipt endpoint. Its original creation payload/key and saved purchase ID are checkpointed in browser local storage per account, including across reloads. Failures remain visible; retry resumes the saved operation. Form fields are locked while an operation is unconfirmed. Do not clear browser storage to recover an uncertain operation.
- Existing orders retain **Receive Stock**. The successful receipt shows its destination and a **View Warehouse Stock** link. No warehouse/ingredient conversion, financial calculation, account grant or historical record is changed by this batch.

Regression checks use mocked storage and mounted routes in `posCheckout.test.ts` and `purchaseStock.test.ts`, including the actual client purchase-submission helper: empty customer collection, stale named customer, durable checkout retry, inline creation, order-only zero stock, 10-bag receipt, failed receiving, lost creation/receipt responses, and repeated receipt without another stock increment. No configured database writes are required.
